G0: A/B harness, golden contract, known-deviations registry, gate evidence

A substrate change of this kind needs a permanent oracle, not a one-off check.
The A/B harness runs the fixed fixture set (Shanghai 2025 Q3 PIA/RUS, Monaco
2025 Q3 NOR/LEC) through both acquisition heads and asserts every Phase 5
expectation programmatically: schema identity against golden snapshots, legacy
byte-identity (flag-isolation proof), exact delta-grid closure, brake-shift
bounds against the LOCAL native sample spacing, registration medians, and the
gap law. Acceptances are encoded in a machine-read registry the harness
enforces: accepted deviations are always rendered (never silently green), hard
failure if one grows beyond its recorded bound, hard failure on anything
unregistered, and a dedicated exit code while owner-pending open findings
exist. Tamper-proofing is verified: deregistering an entry flips the run to
hard failure.

STOP-3 (Shanghai 86ms lap-breakdown closure) was accepted under fork 1 of the
conditional sign-off after a per-zone delta ledger attributed the full
residual within 5ms to two pre-existing excluded-zone mechanisms (T15
zone_too_small stale delta +51.7ms; uncovered head segment -34.1ms). STOP-4
(Monaco brake shifts) was accepted for the three gap-overlapped corners
(T9 Tunnel dropout, T11, T14); T10/T17 are real braking corners outside the
acceptance and remain OPEN findings pending owner ruling.
